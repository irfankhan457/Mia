# Privacy Policy Template

Effective date: Replace before release.

Mia's Fantasy Run is a family-friendly mobile game. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used.

## Information We Collect

The game may collect gameplay and device information through services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Google AdMob, and Google Play Games Services.

This may include:

- App version.
- Device model and operating system.
- Gameplay events.
- Crash diagnostics.
- Advertising identifiers where permitted.
- Google Play Games profile identifiers if the player signs in.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Improve game quality.
- Fix crashes and bugs.
- Measure feature usage.
- Provide leaderboards and achievements.
- Show optional ads where permitted.

## Children And Families

The game is designed to be family-friendly. Configure ads, data collection, and store declarations according to the final target age rating and Google Play Families policy requirements.
