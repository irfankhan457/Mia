# Age Rating Guidance

Expected rating depends on final content and store questionnaire answers.

## Intended Content

- Family-friendly fantasy runner.
- No realistic violence.
- No blood.
- No mature themes.
- No sexualized character design.
- No gambling.
- No user-generated content unless added later.
- Optional ads and optional purchases may be present.

## Questionnaire Notes

- Fantasy obstacles and boss escape moments should be framed as mild non-realistic action.
- If ads are personalized or purchases are enabled, answer the monetization and data sections accurately.
- If targeting children or families, follow Google Play Families policy and ad network requirements.
